The name

Named for a dragon made of fire

Sharpless 113 is a faint cloud of glowing hydrogen in Cygnus, south of the bright star Deneb, known to stargazers as the Flying Dragon.

No. 113 takes its number and its colour: the deep red of hydrogen light. A scent for the hour after dark, when the city goes quiet and the sky gets loud.
